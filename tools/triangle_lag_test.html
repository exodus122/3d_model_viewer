Triangle Lag Test
Click the canvas to fly (WASD + mouse look, Esc to release, Space/C for up/down) - just like the real viewer - so you can reproduce "lags at a certain angle while flying" directly against this isolated wall instead of guessing from a slider. The wall's footprint stays identical as you move Split from 1 to 32 - only triangle count changes, so if GPU time doesn't move, triangle count isn't the variable.
